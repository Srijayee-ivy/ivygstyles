Manyavar is regarded as India's foremost ethnic wear brand that is identical to Indian weddings. Established as a men's ethnic fusion wear designer in 1999, Manyavar slowly ventured into its retail operations in 2001. The one-of-a-kind high-end designs with intricate weaves reflecting Indian culture soon became widely popular making Manyavar a one-stop destination for ethnic celebrations and weddings.

Indian weddings are highly regarded for their grandeur and dazzling charm. Manyavar embraces this tradition by bringing in stunning traditional outfits that will ideally make you wedding-ready and leave others awestruck. Their tailor-made eclectic range lets you radiate Indian elegance with pride. Manyavar celebrates majestic Indian weddings by blending the authenticity of our culture with Indian ethnic attires.

Today, Manyavar has redefined wedding attire with its exclusive and timeless celebration collection ranging from exquisite Sherwanis, Royal Bandhgalad and classy Indo-Westerns to Sleek Kurta-Jackets and fashionable accessories that can uplift your look on your special day. Manyavar has also extended its fashion empire to kids' royal ethnic wear and fusion wear, to give them the best taste of Indian outfits.

Manyavar has draped the world with joy as they have inaugurated 14 international outlets in U.A.E., Canada, the UK and U.S.A. Our iconic attires have a retail presence of approx 1.4 mn sq ft with 640+ EBOs and 5000+ employees associated with our brand. Manyavar is always striving to exceed your expectations just as our brand ambassador Ranveer Singh never fails to amaze us with his remarkable performances. Join Manyavar to create a spark with their exclusive wedding collection and embrace your Indian ethnicity and individuality with style and dignity.
