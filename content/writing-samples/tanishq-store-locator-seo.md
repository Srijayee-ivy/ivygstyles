The word Erode is derived from the Tamil phrase Eeru Odai, which means two streams referring to the two water courses of the state — Pichaikaranpallam and Perumpallam Canal. It is the 7th largest urban agglomeration in Tamil Nadu. It is administered by a municipal corporation since 2008.
